Our Craft & Hospitality

Patience Sealed in Dough, Fragrant With Whole Spices

At The BiryaniWalla, every handi begins long before the doors open for lunch. We parboil aged long-grain basmati rice with green cardamom, shahi jeera, bay leaf, and saffron milk, then layer it over tender marinated cuts inside heavy pots sealed tight with wheat dough.

Alongside our classic Dum Biryanis, our kitchen celebrates authentic Arabian and Hyderabadi Mandi—smoky charcoal-kissed Alfaham, Juicy Chicken, Afghani Mutton, and Ghee Roast platters served generously for solo diners, families, and celebratory groups in Purani Basti, Jharsuguda.
